About Mrs. Clark

A bit more about my background and experience in early childhood education.

Getting to Know Mrs. Clark

Hello, families! My name is Andrea Clark, and I'm very excited to be your child's Preschool Teacher for the 2026–2027 school year. I'm from East Windsor, New Jersey, and I couldn't be more thrilled to begin my third year in the South Brunswick School District.

Starting preschool is an exciting milestone and, for many children, may be their first experience in a school setting. I welcome each child with patience, care, and reassurance while creating a warm, safe, and nurturing classroom where they feel supported, build confidence, and learn and grow at their own pace.

I love working with young children because every day brings new curiosity, laughter, learning, and accomplishments to celebrate. It is a privilege to help children discover new skills, build confidence, and begin developing a lifelong love of learning. Seeing each child grow socially, emotionally, and academically is the most rewarding part of what I do, and I am grateful to be part of their early learning journey.

My teaching goals are to create a safe, nurturing, and inclusive community; promote school readiness through joyful, hands-on play; recognize and support each child's individual strengths and needs; and help children develop independence, communication, empathy, and positive social skills. I take time to get to know each child and practice classroom routines gently and consistently so they can feel secure and successful.
